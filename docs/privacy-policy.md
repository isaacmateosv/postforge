# Privacy information for operators

PostForge is self-hosted. Operators manage their own accounts, credentials, media, and
scheduled content. Connected platforms process data when the operator connects an
account or approves a post. The optional cloud analysis feature sends selected media
only when enabled and invoked.

For provider registration, publish your organization’s own privacy notice on a public
HTTPS URL. Describe the data and services your deployment actually uses, its retention
and deletion practices, and how people can contact your organization.
