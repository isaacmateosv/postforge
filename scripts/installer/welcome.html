Welcome to PostForge __VERSION__

PostForge turns a photo, a video and one sentence into reviewable drafts for your social networks. Nothing is ever published without your approval.

What this installer does

  • Installs PostForge in your home folder at ~/postforge.
  • Installs the PostForge app in ~/Applications and opens it.
  • Upgrades keep your data. Your credentials (.env), Postiz posts, media, logs and settings are never replaced or removed.

What it does not do

  • It does not start any service, link WhatsApp, create drafts or send anything.
  • It does not contain credentials or private data.

PostForge is not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks the app the first time, Control-click it and choose Open.
