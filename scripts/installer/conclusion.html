PostForge __VERSION__ is installed

The project is in ~/postforge and the app is in ~/Applications/PostForge.app. Look for the calendar-and-paper-plane icon in your menu bar.

Next steps:

  1. Put your private .env file in ~/postforge (or use Backup & Move → Restore in the app if you are moving from another Mac).
  2. Open the PostForge menu and choose Turn On.
  3. Choose New Post…, drop a photo or video and write where, when and your idea.

If the app did not open, run ~/postforge/install.sh in Terminal. The install log is at ~/Library/Logs/PostForge-install.log.
